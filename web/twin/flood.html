Read this before believing a number

Two different things are on this page. The cascade responds to the rain you paint (NRCS curve-number runoff routed ward to ward). The learned susceptibility does not respond to rain at all: it is a statistical map of which wards resemble the ones BBMP has flagged.

Ponding is an accumulation index in mm, not a water level. It spreads retained water over a whole ward. Read the ranking, not the value.

The BBMP points have no date, rainfall or depth, and list only places that were flagged, never places confirmed dry. A ward without a point is unlabelled, not safe. They cannot teach any model how much rain causes flooding.

Skill against those labels is modest (see the validation card). Treat both layers as hypotheses to check, not forecasts.
