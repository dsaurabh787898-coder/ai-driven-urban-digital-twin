How to read this

Both scores are 0–100 risks built the same way (hazard, population exposure, vulnerability), but the hazards are different physics, so the two numbers are shown side by side and never averaged.

Flood risk here uses the reference storm (the Flood tab storm) citywide, from the cascade. It is a scenario, not a forecast. Change the storm on the Flood tab and this view follows.

Intervention effects change only the selected ward's own surfaces. Heat uses the learned LST model; flood re-runs the cascade with the ward's imperviousness, canopy and water changed. Water upstream is unchanged, so a ward fed from uphill may improve less than shown.
