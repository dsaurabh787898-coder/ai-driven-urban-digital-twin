Read this before believing a number

This is a cascade mass-balance model over ward polygons, not a hydrodynamic simulation. Runoff comes from the NRCS curve number method, which is published and citable. Routing follows ward-to-ward elevation drops, which is not.

Ponding depth is an accumulation index in millimetres, not a water level. It spreads retained water evenly across a ward of several square kilometres. Real flooding concentrates in a few percent of that area, so actual depth where it floods is many times higher and everywhere else is dry. Read the ranking, not the value.

Flow direction comes from the mean elevation of each ward. A ward whose average sits high can still contain the lowest point around. Real routing follows the storm drain and rajakaluve network, which is culverted in places and runs against the surface gradient.

Nothing here is calibrated against observed flood extents. Doing that needs Sentinel-1 inundation mapping or BBMP incident records, and until then the ward ordering is a hypothesis.
